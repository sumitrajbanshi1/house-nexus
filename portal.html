🧹 House Rules & Maintenance Protocol

  • Kitchen Etiquette & Cooking Cleanliness: Wash up, dry, and put away dishes immediately after eating. Do not leave dirty dishes in the sink overnight. Keep the sink area clean and dry after use. Always wipe down the gas stove, exhaust fan, and surrounding surfaces to remove grease or oil splatters after cooking.
  • Kitchen Trash & Waste: Throw away rubbish immediately. Do not leave plastic bags, egg trays, containers, or empty cardboard boxes lying around the kitchen.
  • Bin Schedule & Maintenance:
    • General Waste: Every Wednesday morning.
    • Recycling (Yellow Bin): Alternate Wednesday morning.
    • Organics/Garden (Green Bin): Alternate Wednesday morning.
    • Daily Upkeep: Empty indoor bins whenever you see they are full—do not pile trash above the rim.
  • Quiet Hours: Maintain low volume between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM out of respect for working housemates.
  • House Cleaning: All household members are equally responsible for keeping shared areas clean. Vacuum floors whenever they look dirty or dusty.
  • Shared Bathroom: Clean up hair from drains immediately after showering, wipe down surfaces, and replace toilet rolls when finished.
  • Overnight Guests: Allowed up to 2 consecutive nights. Notify housemates via group text beforehand.

📜 Terms & Conditions

  • Bond Payment: Security deposit equals 1 month's rent, payable prior to moving in.
  • Bond Refund: The full bond amount will be returned upon move-out, provided the property is left in the same clean, undamaged condition as when handed over.
  • Property Damage: If damage occurs to the property beyond normal wear and tear, the tenant responsible must cover the full cost of repair, even if it exceeds the bond amount.
  • Notice Period: A minimum of 4 weeks' written notice is required before vacating the property.
